# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Nigerian businesses (startups and growing companies) and individuals seeking legal services — corporate advisory, contract drafting/review, litigation and dispute resolution, real estate, employment/HR, regulatory compliance, IP, and family/personal matters. Based in Jos, Plateau State; clients span across Nigeria and beyond.

Context when visiting: evaluating whether to trust an unfamiliar firm before reaching out, often for the first time, sometimes under time pressure (a dispute, a filing deadline, a business decision that can't wait). Many are unfamiliar with legal process and want plain guidance on what happens next, not jargon.

The separate `/admin` dashboard (blog + submissions management) is a distinct product-register surface used internally by the firm, not by these visitors.

## Product Purpose

Convert visitors into qualified leads — consultation requests and contact-form submissions — by establishing credibility fast and removing friction from "I have a legal problem" to "I've reached out." The blog exists to support this via ongoing SEO/content credibility. Success looks like: submitted contact forms, scheduled consultations, and newsletter signups that convert into consultations over time.

## Brand Personality

Confident & Approachable. Practical and momentum-focused rather than exhaustive; human, not robotic. Voice is clear and direct — explains things plainly, never hides behind jargon — and reassuring without being saccharine. Serious subject matter (disputes, compliance, contracts) handled with warmth, not intimidation.

## Anti-references

- Generic stock-photo law firm sites: gavel/scales-of-justice imagery, navy-and-gold "trust us" templates, wall-of-text about pages.
- Generic AI/SaaS-startup template look: gradient text, hero-metric blocks, tiny uppercase eyebrow kickers on every section, identical icon-tile card grids.
- Intimidating, overly formal, or bureaucratic tone — the firm should feel like a capable partner, not a wall.

## Design Principles

1. **Reduce uncertainty at every step.** Clear next steps over legal jargon — what happens after you submit the form, what an "urgent" flag does, what to expect from a consultation.
2. **Practice what you preach.** A firm that wants clients to feel confident should itself look confident — no generic templates standing in for that confidence.
3. **Show, don't tell.** Real testimonials, concrete per-service descriptions, and named team bios carry the credibility claims, not adjectives alone.
4. **Human, not robotic.** Approachable tone throughout, even while covering formal subject matter — this is a standing anti-pattern check, not a one-time pass.
5. **Momentum over exhaustiveness.** Prioritize the next action (schedule a consultation, get in touch) over decorative or filler content.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA as the target: body text ≥4.5:1 contrast, keyboard navigability, visible focus states. Every animation needs a `prefers-reduced-motion` fallback (already the pattern for the hero stats and the trusted-companies logo marquee) — this applies to all future motion work, not just what exists today. No unusual requirements beyond that.
